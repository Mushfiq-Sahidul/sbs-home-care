FAQ

Common questions

What does non-medical home care mean?
It means help with everyday living, like company, meals, housekeeping, and personal care. It does not include nursing or medical treatment.
How do I get started?
Call us at (347) 364-7775 or send a message. We will talk about what you need and explain the next steps.
Do you offer flexible schedules?
We can talk about hourly visits, live-in care, and short breaks. What we can offer depends on caregiver availability.
Does insurance or Medicaid pay for home care?
Payment options are different for each family. Please call us to talk about your situation, and check with your insurance provider about coverage.
Can family help shape the care plan?
Yes. Family input helps us understand routines, likes, and what matters most.
What areas do you serve?
We serve families across Connecticut. Please call to confirm your town.
