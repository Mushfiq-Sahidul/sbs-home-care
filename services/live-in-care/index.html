What is live-in care?

A caregiver stays in the home on an agreed schedule and gives non-medical help.

Live-in is not the same as 24-hour awake care. Hours, rest breaks, and night-time help are agreed before we start.

Services may include

  • Help with daily routines
  • Friendly company
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Simple meal preparation
  • Personal care, when agreed in the plan

Who may benefit?

People who want to stay at home
People who need help at several times a day
People who enjoy having company
Families wanting a steady plan

A steady, familiar presence can bring comfort to the whole household.

Questions

Common questions

Is live-in care the same as 24-hour care?
No. A live-in caregiver has rest breaks and sleep time. Round-the-clock awake care is different.
What should we plan before starting?
The schedule, duties, night-time needs, breaks, meals, sleeping space, backup coverage, and cost.
How much does live-in care cost?
It depends on the schedule and duties. Please call us to talk about your situation.
