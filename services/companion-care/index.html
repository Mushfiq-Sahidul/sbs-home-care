What is companion care?

Companion care is friendly company and non-medical help at home.

It is about talking, sharing activities, and keeping familiar routines.

Services may include

  • Friendly conversation
  • Games, puzzles, music, and hobbies
  • Walks and simple outings
  • Company at mealtimes
  • Light household help and errands, when agreed

Who may benefit?

Older adults who live alone
People who enjoy company
Busy families
People with favorite hobbies

Company, kindness, and a friendly face can make every day feel better.

— SBS Home Care
Questions

Common questions

Does companion care include personal care?
Companion care is mostly company and everyday help. Personal care is a separate service, so we talk about it on its own.
Can a companion help with errands?
Sometimes. Simple errands or shopping can be added when agreed in advance.
Is companion care available across Connecticut?
We serve families across Connecticut. Please call to confirm your town and schedule.
