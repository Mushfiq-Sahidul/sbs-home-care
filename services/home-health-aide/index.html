What is non-medical home aide support?

It is practical help with everyday tasks around the home.

We do not provide nursing, medical treatment, or medication help. If your loved one needs medical care, please ask their doctor about a licensed home health agency.

Services may include

  • Light housekeeping
  • Laundry and changing linens
  • Simple meal preparation
  • Grocery shopping and errands
  • Help keeping the home organized

Who may benefit?

Older adults
People who find housework hard
People who want an orderly home
Families needing practical help

A tidy, comfortable home helps everyday life feel calmer.

Questions

Common questions

Is a home aide the same as a home health aide?
Not always. In Connecticut, medical home health care comes from agencies licensed for it. We provide non-medical help with daily living.
Do you provide medical care?
No. We do not provide nursing, medical treatment, medication administration, or wound care.
Can you help with cooking and laundry?
Yes, simple meals, laundry, and light housekeeping can be part of the plan.
