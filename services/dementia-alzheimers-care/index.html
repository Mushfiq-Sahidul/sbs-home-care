What is non-medical dementia support?

It is patient help with daily life and friendly company at home.

We do not diagnose, treat, or manage any medical condition. Questions about health go to your loved one's doctor.

Services may include

  • Familiar daily routines
  • Calm, patient company
  • Help with everyday activities
  • Talking with the family about routines and preferences
  • Activities your loved one enjoys

Who may benefit?

People with memory changes
People who like familiar routines
Families wanting a patient helper
Caregivers needing a break

Every person has a story. We take time to learn it and treat each person with respect.

Common questions

Do you diagnose or treat Alzheimer's disease?
No. We provide non-medical support only. We do not diagnose, treat, or manage any medical condition.
Can families share routines and preferences?
Yes. Family details help us plan kind, familiar support.
Does non-medical care replace medical care?
No. It does not replace a doctor, clinical care, or emergency services.
