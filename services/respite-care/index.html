What is respite care?

Respite care is short-term, non-medical help that gives family caregivers a break.

It can be a few hours, an afternoon, or another time we agree on.

Services may include

  • Friendly company
  • Following your loved one's routine
  • Light household help
  • Personal care, when agreed in the plan
  • Time for your errands, appointments, or rest

Who may benefit?

Family caregivers
People with busy schedules
Families planning a trip or event
Anyone who needs time to rest

Taking a break is a normal part of caring for someone you love.

— SBS Home Care
Questions

Common questions

How early should I ask for respite care?
As early as you can. Availability varies, so call with your dates and hours.
Can respite care become regular care?
Possibly. Some families start with a short break and later set up a regular schedule.
Does respite care include medical care?
No. We provide non-medical help only.
