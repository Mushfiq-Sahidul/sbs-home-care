How to Talk to a Parent Who Resists Help

"I don't need anyone." If you have heard this, you are not alone. It is often about independence and pride, not about you.

By SBS Home CareOctober 29, 20262 min read

Listen first

Ask, "What part of the day is hardest?" Listening helps your parent feel heard, not managed.

Talk about what matters to them

Link help to their goals, like staying in their own home. "Let someone help with laundry so you have energy for the garden" works better than "you can't keep up."

Start small

A few hours of companion care or help with errands is a gentle first step.

Offer choices

Let your parent help choose the schedule and the tasks. It becomes their plan, not yours.

Try it for a short time

"Let's try it for a few weeks" feels easier than a permanent change. Respite care is one short-term option.

Be patient

It may take several talks. Avoid ultimatums. If you want to talk it through, call us at (347) 364-7775.
