Live-In Care vs. Hourly Care: Which Fits Your Family?

Should a caregiver visit for set hours, or stay in the home? The answer depends on your loved one's day and your family's needs.

By SBS Home CareOctober 29, 20262 min read

Hourly care

A caregiver visits for an agreed number of hours. This suits people who want help at certain times, like mornings, meals, or errands.

Live-in care

A live-in caregiver stays in the home on an agreed schedule. Live-in does not mean 24-hour awake care. Rest breaks and night-time arrangements are planned in advance.

A quick comparison

Hourly careLive-in care
Caregiver is thereDuring visitsOn an agreed schedule at home
Best forHelp at certain timesHelp across more of the day
Night-timeNot usually includedMust be agreed in advance
FlexibilityEasy to adjustNeeds more planning

Questions to ask first

  • What help is needed, and at what times?
  • What if help is needed at night?
  • Where will the caregiver sleep and eat?
  • What is the backup plan?
  • What will it cost?

Which to try first

If your loved one mainly needs company and light help, hourly companion care may be enough. If constant awake help is needed, ask their doctor what is right. Call (347) 364-7775 to talk it through.

Common questions

Is live-in care the same as 24-hour care?
No. A live-in caregiver has breaks and sleep time. Round-the-clock awake care is different.
Can we switch from hourly to live-in later?
Often, yes. Call us to talk about availability and planning.
