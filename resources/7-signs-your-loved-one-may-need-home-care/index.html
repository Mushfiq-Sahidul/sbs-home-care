7 Signs Your Loved One May Need Home Care

Most families notice small changes first. Here are seven everyday signs that extra help at home may be worth talking about.

By SBS Home CareOctober 29, 20262 min read

1. Changes in appearance or hygiene

Wearing the same clothes, or less grooming than before, can mean daily routines are getting tiring. Personal care can help in a respectful way.

2. A home that is harder to keep up

Piles of laundry, dishes in the sink, or unopened mail can be a sign. Home aide support can help with light housekeeping.

3. Changes in eating

An empty refrigerator or spoiled food may mean shopping and cooking are getting hard. Questions about diet belong with their doctor.

4. Spending more time alone

Seeing friends less often can feel lonely. A friendly companion can bring conversation back into the week.

5. Trouble keeping up with errands and routines

Missed appointments or unpaid bills are common reasons families look for help. We do not manage medications. For medication questions, please talk to their doctor or pharmacist.

6. Moving around seems harder

If everyday movement seems tiring, a doctor can check for health causes. A caregiver can help with daily routines at home.

7. The family caregiver is worn out

Sometimes the biggest sign is how the family feels. Respite care gives caregivers a break.

What to do next

  1. Start a gentle, caring conversation. Listen first.
  2. Talk to their doctor about any health worries.
  3. Write down what you have noticed.
  4. Call us. We will talk through what help might look like.

Common questions

How do I know if my parent needs home care?
There is no single test. If you notice several of these signs, it may be time to talk about extra help.
Is home care the same as medical care?
No. Non-medical home care helps with daily living and company. It is not nursing or medical treatment.
