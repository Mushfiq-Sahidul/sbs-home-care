The Real Benefits of In-Home Care vs. Facility Care

Choosing between help at home and a care facility is a big decision. Neither is right for everyone. Here is a simple comparison.

By SBS Home CareOctober 29, 20262 min read

Comfort and routine

Many people want to stay in the home they know, with their own things and their own daily routine. Home care brings the help to them.

Privacy and family time

At home, a caregiver's time is for one household. Family can visit any time and stay close to daily life.

Cost

Costs vary a lot. Home care is usually priced by the hour or by schedule. Facilities often charge a monthly rate with extras. Always ask for a written price list and compare the same things.

What each can provide

SBS Home Care provides non-medical help only. Facilities may have staff on site at all hours, and some provide medical care. If your loved one needs medical care or constant supervision, ask their doctor what is right.

A quick comparison

Care at homeCare facility
WhereTheir own homeA residential community
ScheduleBased on their routineOften a shared schedule
CostUsually by the hourOften a monthly rate
Medical careNot provided by SBS Home CareVaries by facility

You can mix both

Many families start with a few hours of companion care and change the plan over time.

Common questions

Is in-home care cheaper than a facility?
It depends on the hours and services. Ask each provider for a written estimate.
Can non-medical home care replace a facility?
Not always. It does not include nursing or constant supervision. Ask their doctor what level of care fits.
Please note: SBS Home Care provides non-medical home care only. This article is general information, not medical, legal, or financial advice. For health questions, please talk with a doctor.
