How to Choose a Home Care Agency in Connecticut

Searching for home care returns many options, and they are not all the same. Use this simple checklist to compare agencies.

By SBS Home CareOctober 29, 20262 min read

Know what kind of help you need

In Connecticut, non-medical help (company, housekeeping, errands) usually comes from homemaker-companion agencies registered with the Department of Consumer Protection. Medical home health care comes from agencies licensed by the Department of Public Health. Ask every agency which kind it is.

Check registration or license

Ask for the agency's registration or license details and check them with the state. Interview more than one agency before you sign.

Questions to ask

  • Are caregivers employees of the agency?
  • How are caregivers screened and trained?
  • Will I get a written service plan and price list?
  • What happens if a caregiver is sick?
  • How will you keep our family informed?

Warning signs

  • Pressure to sign right away.
  • Unclear answers about registration or screening.
  • No written agreement.
  • Promises that sound too good to be true.

Common questions

What is the difference between home care and home health care?
Home care is non-medical help with daily living. Home health care is medical care from licensed professionals.
How can I check an agency in Connecticut?
Ask for its registration or license details and check them with the Connecticut state department that oversees it.
