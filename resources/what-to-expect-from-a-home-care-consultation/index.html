What to Expect From a Home Care Consultation

Calling a home care agency for the first time can feel like a big step. Here is what usually happens, in plain steps.

By SBS Home CareOctober 29, 20262 min read

Step 1: Contact us

Call (347) 364-7775 or send a message. We will ask who needs help and what you are hoping for.

Step 2: Talk with us

We talk about your loved one's routine and the help you are considering, like companionship or personal care. We explain what we do and do not provide.

Step 3: A simple written plan

We write down the tasks, schedule, and arrangement so everyone knows what to expect before care begins.

Step 4: Care begins

We discuss the caregiver and start date, and we stay in touch to make changes as needed. Availability varies.

How to prepare

  • Think about which parts of the day are hardest.
  • Write down routines and favorite things.
  • Decide what help you want and when.
  • Bring your questions, including about cost.

Common questions

Is the first conversation a medical evaluation?
No. We provide non-medical services. We talk about daily routines and practical needs, not medical care.
Does the consultation cost anything?
Please call us to ask about current details.
Please note: SBS Home Care provides non-medical home care only. This article is general information, not medical, legal, or financial advice. For health questions, please talk with a doctor.
